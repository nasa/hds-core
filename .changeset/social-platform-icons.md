Add 23 social and platform marks to the HDS sprite, plus a `mail` glyph.

**Added:** `logo-*` marks for the platforms where NASA has official accounts, the share targets used on nasa.gov and NASA+, and the platforms NASA publishes apps, podcasts, and code on. Use them with `.hds-btn-icon--social`. Sources and licenses are listed in `docs/CREDITS.md`.

**Fixed:** glyphs in `.hds-btn-icon--social` now render at 70% of the button instead of 60%, matching the Figma social spec. Existing social buttons get slightly larger icons; no markup change is needed.
